Codelists used for CPRD protocol 20_00061: Outcome of fractures in people with intellectual disabilities

The hip fracture cohort had icd10 codes S72.0,S72.1,S72.2 or CPRD Aurum codes mapping to these ICD10 codes, see fracture code list.

Codelists named CAM* were based on the Cambridge codelists of the QOF v44 Business Rules mapped to CPRD Aurum.
